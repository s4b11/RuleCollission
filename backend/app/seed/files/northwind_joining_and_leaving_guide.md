Northwind Labs (fictional) — Joining and Leaving Guide

This guide is synthetic demonstration material. It is not the policy of any real company.

1. Who does what

Human Resources keeps the exit record, issues employee IDs, joining letters and relieving letters. The IT Service Desk issues laptops and gives IT asset clearance. Finance / Payroll prepares the full-and-final settlement. Admin & Facilities collects access cards and issues the no-dues certificate. The Reporting Manager accepts resignations. Legal handles the NDA. Background Verification (Security) checks new joiners.

2. Leaving the company

Send your resignation letter to your reporting manager, who accepts it. Return your access card to Admin & Facilities to receive the no-dues certificate, which some teams still call the clearance form. IT gives asset clearance only after HR has opened your exit record, and HR opens the exit record only after IT asset clearance. Because these two wait for each other, HR may approve a provisional exit ticket once your resignation is accepted. IT may accept that ticket in place of the exit record. Finance settles your dues after the exit record and the no-dues certificate. HR issues the relieving letter after the settlement.

3. Joining the company

After you sign the offer, Background Verification checks your documents and HR issues your joining letter. HR issues your employee ID after the NDA is signed on the e-sign portal. Legal opens the e-sign portal only on a company laptop, and IT issues a laptop only against an employee ID. Because these three wait for each other, Legal may approve a wet-ink paper NDA, which HR may accept in place of the e-signed NDA.

4. Who can approve exceptions

Only HR approves a provisional exit ticket and only IT accepts it. Only Legal approves a paper NDA and only HR accepts it. The people desk assistant explains the process and asks the right office; it cannot approve anything.
