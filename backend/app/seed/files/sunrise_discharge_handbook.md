Sunrise General Hospital (fictional) — Discharge and Cashless Claims Handbook

This handbook is synthetic demonstration material. It is not the policy of any real hospital, insurer or claims administrator.

1. Who does what

The Ward / Nursing Station records the admission note on arrival and issues the discharge authorisation at the end of the stay. The Treating Doctor writes the discharge summary once the laboratory summary is ready. The Laboratory prepares the laboratory summary after admission. The Billing Department prepares bills and issues billing clearance. The Hospital Insurance Desk helps families with cashless paperwork. MediLink TPA reviews cashless claims on behalf of the insurer.

2. Cashless pre-authorisation

Pre-authorisation is requested from MediLink TPA soon after admission. From 1 April 2026 the TPA needs only the admission note and the insurance policy e-card. The room-rent declaration form was retired on 31 March 2026 and must not be requested any more. A pre-authorisation is valid for the approved number of days. If the stay is extended beyond that, the pre-authorisation expires and a fresh one must be obtained before billing can continue.

3. Final bill and final cashless approval

The Billing Department issues the final hospital bill after the pre-authorisation and the final cashless approval are both available. MediLink TPA issues the final cashless approval after it receives the final hospital bill and the discharge summary.

Because each of these two documents waits for the other, the hospital has an approved alternative. The Billing Department may issue an interim (provisional) bill once pre-authorisation exists. The interim bill can be used in place of the final bill for the TPA's final approval, but only when a Billing approver approves it for that patient and the TPA agrees to accept it for that patient. The approval applies to one patient and one admission only.

4. Billing clearance and discharge

Billing clearance is issued after the final hospital bill. The Ward issues the discharge authorisation, also called the discharge pass, when both billing clearance and the discharge summary are available.

5. Names for documents

MediLink TPA sometimes asks for an "investigation report". Families should ask the TPA whether the laboratory summary is what they mean; only the TPA can confirm that.

6. Who can approve exceptions

Nursing staff can explain the process but cannot approve billing exceptions. Only a Billing approver can approve an interim bill. Only the TPA can accept an interim bill for final approval. If no approved alternative applies, the Hospital Administrator decides.

7. Visiting hours and help

Visiting hours are 10:00 to 12:00 and 16:00 to 19:00. The help desk beside Main Reception is open from 08:00 to 20:00. Pharmacy counters close at 22:00.
