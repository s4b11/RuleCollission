Sunrise General Hospital (fictional) — Visitor and Billing Questions

This page is synthetic demonstration material. It is not the policy of any real hospital, insurer or claims administrator.

1. Visiting hours

General wards are open to visitors from 10:00 to 12:00 and from 16:00 to 19:00. The cardiac ward allows one visitor at a time. The maternity ward allows the partner at any time and other visitors in the afternoon slot.

2. Where to go

The Main Reception is at the front entrance. The Billing Counter is on the ground floor next to the pharmacy. The Insurance Desk is beside the Billing Counter and helps with cashless paperwork. Each ward has its own nursing station.

3. Paying a deposit

Patients without cashless insurance pay a deposit at the Billing Counter on admission. Patients with an approved cashless pre-authorisation do not pay a deposit, except for items the policy does not cover, such as personal toiletries or a room upgrade.

4. How long discharge takes

A planned discharge usually takes three to five hours after the doctor signs the discharge summary. Most of that time is spent on the final bill and the insurer's final approval. Families can ask the discharge assistant at any time what is still pending and which desk to visit.

5. If the stay is extended

If the doctor extends the stay beyond the days approved in the pre-authorisation, the Insurance Desk asks MediLink TPA for an enhancement. Until it arrives, the pre-authorisation counts as expired and billing cannot continue.

6. Getting copies

Copies of the discharge summary and laboratory summary are given with the discharge pass. Further copies can be requested from the Main Reception within 30 days.
